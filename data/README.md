## Market input data

`VIX_data.csv`, `VVIX_data.csv`, and `SVXY_data.csv` are datasets downloaded from Yahoo Finance through `yfinance` library. This data was generated by [the retrieval notebook](../notebooks/00_vix_vvix_svxy_data_retrieval.ipynb).

`SPVIXSTR.xlsx` is the manually downloaded [Investing.com SPVIXSTR history](https://www.investing.com/indices/sp-500-vix-short-term-futures-tri-historical-data) used by both synthetic notebooks. SPVIXSTR is the total-return version of the S&P 500 VIX Short-Term Futures Index. It represents rolling short-term VIX futures exposure and includes a collateral-return component. See the [S&P index methodology](https://www.spglobal.com/spdji/en/documents/methodologies/methodology-sp-vix-futures-indices.pdf).

## Cboe F1/F2 history

[01_VIX_Futures_CBOE_F1_F2.ipynb](../notebooks/01_VIX_Futures_CBOE_F1_F2.ipynb) downloads monthly VX histories from [Cboe](https://www.cboe.com/markets/us/futures/market-statistics/historical-data/futures/). Then it constructs F1/F2 from the first two available monthly contracts.

F1 and F2 contain daily settlement prices. Prices before **March 26, 2007** are divided by ten, following the [Cboe contract rescaling](https://cdn.cboe.com/resources/regulation/circulars/general/CFE-IC-2007-003.pdf), to obtain a consistent history.

Although not used in this study, `Days_to_exp1` and `Days_to_exp2` follow the **VolChart.io convention**: the third Friday of the month after the contract month, minus 31 calendar days, minus the observation date.

## SVXY synthetic scenarios

Both synthetic notebooks take `SVXY_data.csv` and `SPVIXSTR.xlsx` as inputs to approximate consistent −1x and −0.5x daily exposures time series. The methodologies used are sufficiently explained in the comments of their respective notebooks.
