## Market input data

`VIX_data.csv`, `VVIX_data.csv`, and `SVXY_data.csv` are datasets downloaded from Yahoo Finance through `yfinance` library. This data was generated by [the retrieval notebook](../notebooks/00_vix_vvix_svxy_data_retrieval.ipynb).

**VVIX data note**: The Yahoo Finance VVIX dataset had eight missing observations. We added only their Close values manually from Cboe's historical data, without interpolation. Open, High, and Low remain blank for those dates. This manual addition is not included in the retrieval notebook's download code.

`SPVIXSTR.xlsx` is the manually downloaded [Investing.com SPVIXSTR history](https://www.investing.com/indices/sp-500-vix-short-term-futures-tri-historical-data) used by both synthetic notebooks. SPVIXSTR is the total-return version of the S&P 500 VIX Short-Term Futures Index. It represents rolling short-term VIX futures exposure and includes a collateral-return component. See the [S&P index methodology](https://www.spglobal.com/spdji/en/documents/methodologies/methodology-sp-vix-futures-indices.pdf).

## Cboe F1/F2 history

[01_VIX_Futures_CBOE_F1_F2.ipynb](../notebooks/01_VIX_Futures_CBOE_F1_F2.ipynb) downloads monthly VX histories from [Cboe](https://www.cboe.com/markets/us/futures/market-statistics/historical-data/futures/). Then it constructs F1/F2 from the first two available monthly contracts.

F1 and F2 contain daily settlement prices. Prices before **March 26, 2007** are divided by ten, following the [Cboe contract rescaling](https://cdn.cboe.com/resources/regulation/circulars/general/CFE-IC-2007-003.pdf), to obtain a consistent history.

Although not used in this study, `Days_to_exp1` and `Days_to_exp2` follow the **VolChart.io convention**: the third Friday of the month after the contract month, minus 31 calendar days, minus the observation date.

## SVXY synthetic scenarios

The [−1x synthetic notebook](../notebooks/02_Synthetic_SVXY-ONEx.ipynb) and the [−0.5x synthetic notebook](../notebooks/03_Synthetic_SVXY-HALFx.ipynb) take `SVXY_data.csv` and `SPVIXSTR.xlsx` as inputs to approximate consistent −1x and −0.5x daily exposure time series. The methodologies used are explained in the comments of their respective notebooks.

## Trading system inputs

The [−1x SMA notebook](../notebooks/05_SMA_crossover_trading_system.ipynb) uses `SVXY_synth-ONEx.csv`, and the [−0.5x SMA notebook](../notebooks/06_SMA_crossover_trading_system-HALFx.ipynb) uses `SVXY_synth-HALFx.csv`.

[Model 2073 — Synthetic SVXY −1x](../notebooks/07-Model_2073_SVXY-1.ipynb) uses the `Date` and `Close` columns from [raw/VIX_data.csv](raw/VIX_data.csv) and [processed/SVXY_synth-ONEx.csv](processed/SVXY_synth-ONEx.csv), through September 16, 2026. To run notebook 07, place both files in the same folder as the notebook, keeping their existing filenames. VVIX and the futures curve are not inputs to this model.

The model's performance tables and plots are stored in notebook 07. They cover Development, Validation, Test and a continuous full-history simulation, each compared with buy and hold.
